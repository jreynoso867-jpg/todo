                                        Your list in one file

                                        Save everything (tasks, lists, clients, templates, shopping lists, wishes, savings goals, important dates and photos) in one file. Keep it as a backup, or load it on a new phone to bring everything along.

                                        Safety copies

                                        Your whole list (everything but the photos) is copied on this phone each day you use the app, and before every update. If something ever goes wrong, put a copy back.

                                          Passcode lock

                                          Ask for a 6-digit passcode when the app opens, and again after a minute away. Your list is hidden while you switch apps. The passcode isn’t encryption: it stops people opening your list here, but the list is kept on this phone as it is, guarded by the phone’s own passcode, and anyone with a Save everything file can read it.
