# Tests

Browser tests with Playwright (Chromium), on an iPhone-sized screen. Each test gets its own fresh storage.

```sh
cd tests && npm install          # once: installs Playwright
npx playwright install chromium  # once, if you don't have its Chromium yet
cd ..
node --test --test-concurrency=1 tests/repo.test.cjs tests/upgrade.test.cjs tests/features.test.cjs tests/regression.test.cjs tests/native.test.cjs tests/artifact.test.cjs tests/fixes.test.cjs tests/sections.test.cjs tests/tabs.test.cjs tests/shopping.test.cjs tests/wishes.test.cjs tests/goals.test.cjs tests/dates.test.cjs tests/daily.test.cjs
```

- `repo.test.cjs`: the repository is public, so every kind of private file (the Claude edition with your own words, transcripts, files the app saves, backups, signing keys and certificates, Xcode output) is kept out of commits by `.gitignore`, nothing the app needs is, and no committed file holds a key or token. No browser; it skips outside a git checkout.
- `upgrade.test.cjs`: data preservation. Fills the phone's storage the way the previous version keeps it (every task field, lists, categories, clients, templates, trash, history, settings, a photo), opens the old version, then the new one on the same storage, and checks that every record and photo is byte-for-byte unchanged, that only new settings were added, that the upgrade safety copy holds the whole list, and that every task still shows. `OLD_REF` picks the old version (a git ref; default `fd3731e`, the last version before 2.0).
- `features.test.cjs`: the bug fixes and new features, plus restart, no connection, a save the phone refuses, an empty list, export and import, the largest text on a small iPhone, and contrast in every theme (Blush measured at its gradient's strongest point; the other looks' colors checked against the version before Blush, `PRE_BLUSH_REF`, all but High priority's, which is violet now).
- `native.test.cjs`: the iPhone app, with a stand-in for its native side (`fake-capacitor.cjs`, shaped like Capacitor's iOS bridge): reminders become notifications at the right moments and within iOS's limit of 64, they're taken back when a task is done or reminders are off, a tapped notification opens its task, files go through the share menu, the daily safety copy in Files, photos, haptics, the status bar, and the website without the shell.
- `artifact.test.cjs`: the Claude edition, built by `tools/build-artifact.cjs`, on a stand-in for Claude's runtime (only `window.claude.use`): the list loads and nothing in it changes, the Claude-only parts show (Google Calendar, asking Claude, Save everything through Claude's downloads), a new task survives a reload, Claude's dark switch is followed, a look saved by the old version comes through, and the owner's own words are carried over from the live copy instead of living in this repository.
- `fixes.test.cjs`: problems found by the October review, each written to fail before its fix: unchecking a repeating task (an edited next copy stays; an untouched one goes to Recently deleted), a repeating PO marked received, a repeating job keeping its client, Clear done in a day view, a delete whose Recently deleted copy can't be kept, a safety copy before each new version, and more.
- `sections.test.cjs`: the newer kinds of record (shopping lists and their items, wishes, savings goals and their entries, important dates) and their Recently deleted (`bin/`, never the tasks' `trash/`): Save everything and Load a saved file carry every one, a second load adds nothing; Recently deleted shows them by name and kind, brings a list back with its items and a goal with its entries exactly as they were, and clears them after 7 days or with Delete all now; a save cut off by closing goes back in, a save the phone can't make yet waits and goes in later; the iPhone app's Files copy holds them. The money, link and date helpers and moving records into Recently deleted (no screens yet) run in Node straight from index.html. `upgrade.test.cjs` also checks that the version before them (`PRE_SECTIONS_REF`, default `58a3ff2`) leaves them alone.
- `tabs.test.cjs`: the tab bar (Home, Tasks, Lists, Calendar, Settings): one place shows at a time and the one tapped is kept on the phone (never just for opening), everything the app had before is two taps or less from Home, the bar can't be reached behind a sheet, the Tasks button counts what's overdue, nothing scrolls sideways at 320 points with Extra large text on any tab, and what's on each tab (Home's agenda with circles to tick, the Tasks summary and list picker, a card per list, the calendar's day taps and "+ New event"). The other tests open on Tasks (`todo.tab` in the fixtures); `artifact.test.cjs` checks the tabs in the Claude edition too.
- `shopping.test.cjs`: shopping lists on the Lists tab, made and used by tapping: a new list and its items are saved as the records above and nothing else changes (the task box included); a card shows the first four things to get and "+N"; ticking moves an item under Done and is kept after a reload; Clear done and deleting a list (⋮) go to Recently deleted with Undo, and Restore brings the list and its items back exactly; editing an item (its words, a link that must be a web link, its place, delete); a pasted link becomes the item's link and opens in a new tab with nothing passed on; markup and emoji show as words; a save the phone can't keep leaves no ghost; Back, Escape and other tabs close the open list, N adds there, the add bar is out of reach behind a sheet; no sideways scrolling at 320 points with Extra large text.
- `wishes.test.cjs`: the Wish list, opened from its card on the Lists tab and used by tapping: a new wish is saved as the record above (price in whole cents, the link https only) and nothing else changes; the heart is kept at once and after a reload; a tapped card opens its sheet filled in; a `javascript:` (or any other non-web) link and a price that isn't one are refused; markup and emoji show as words; the chips (All, Travel, Food, Plans, Shopping, Other) show one kind and are kept for the visit only; prices read "$8,999.00"; Got it folds a wish under Got it and Not yet brings it back; Delete goes to Recently deleted with Undo, and Restore there brings it back exactly; Back, Escape and other tabs close it, N adds a wish, nothing behind its sheet can be reached; no sideways scrolling at 320 points with Extra large text; the raspberry kind, the heart and the link read clearly on a Blush card (light and dark) as it really shows.
- `goals.test.cjs`: Savings goals, opened from their card on the Lists tab (next to the Wish list's, under "Wishes and savings") and used by tapping: a new goal and money put into it are saved as the records above (whole cents; what's saved and what's left are never stored) and nothing else changes; a goal of $1,000.00 with $250.00 in reads "$250.00 of $1,000.00", "$750.00 to go" and a bar at 25%, after a reload too; 12.10 and 0.20 make $12.30; Take out (or a minus sign) lowers what's saved, and zero or not-an-amount is refused; "Reached!" at the target or over it; the due date reads "Jan 4, 2027" and what went in shows newest first; Close goal moves a goal under Closed and keeps it all, Reopen (or Undo) brings it back; an entry tapped can be changed or deleted, with Undo; deleting a goal takes its entries along as one row in Recently deleted, and Restore brings them all back exactly; markup and emoji show as words; Back, Escape and other tabs close it, N adds a goal, nothing behind its sheets can be reached; no sideways scrolling at 320 points with Extra large text; the bar and the money read clearly on a Blush card (light and dark) as it really shows.
- `dates.test.cjs`: important dates and the rest of the Calendar tab, and Home's newer cards, with the page's clock set by the test so every countdown is exact. Calendar opens on the month (the week to plan is a tap away there, and Week is kept only when picked); under the month, "Dates to remember" lists the next dates soonest first with each kind's icon and "Today" (on its own pill), "Tomorrow", "In N days" up to 60, then the day ("Dec 16", with the year once it's next year), the one-off dates already gone folded under Past dates; a birthday just after New Year, and a Feb 29 birthday on Feb 28 in other years and on Feb 29 in a leap year; dots under the days with open tasks, dates (yearly ones every year) and open goals' due dates, and a day tapped still opens it in Tasks; "Everything this month" by day (a task opens it, a date its sheet, a goal Savings goals); a date made, changed and deleted by tapping is saved as its record (Every year on for a birthday or anniversary until set by hand), goes to Recently deleted with Undo, and Restore brings it back exactly; the sheet's Escape, Cancel, tap outside and N; Home's Coming up (the next three dates; to the Calendar's dates), Savings (the open goals' bars; to Savings goals) and New for you (the Wish list; Dismiss is kept on the phone only when tapped, and opening the app writes nothing), each hidden with nothing to show; markup and emoji as words; no sideways scrolling at 320 points with Extra large text; the countdowns, icons and dots on a Blush card (light and dark) as it really shows, and New for you on its wash. `native.test.cjs` checks the iPhone app's morning notes for dates (their own ids, tasks first under the limit of 60, none with reminders Off, a tap opens the Calendar).
- `daily.test.cjs`: the daily workflow. A repeating task that's only put off (Move to today, Move to tomorrow on Up next, Select's Tomorrow or Date, a drag in the week planner) keeps its schedule: it notes the day it was due (`postponed: { from, to }`), so done late it comes back on its usual day (a weekly timesheet moved from last Wednesday to today is next due tomorrow, Wednesday, not a week from today; a monthly bill keeps its day of the month). Undo puts the note back as it was; a date changed in the task itself moves the schedule to the new day, as before, and a note that no longer matches the task's day (another copy of the app moved it) is ignored. Home's agenda: Overdue (the most important first, then the oldest; a Today button on each, and Move all N to today), Today (a date first, then by time), Next 7 days (tasks and dates by day; a week off isn't there yet) and Waiting on (who and how long; "Follow up with" once its day has come), the first few of each with "N more" opening Tasks on just that view, the tasks with no date counted at the end; opening Home writes nothing; with nothing due it says so. Every count of a part of the list agrees: a section's heading counts the task on Up next too, the same as the views, the badge and Move all N to today; a late task swiped left offers Today (keeping a repeating one's schedule), and on a small iPhone its four choices still leave some of the task showing. Adding from Home takes one tap and the words: smart add reads the day, time, priority and list ("Call the bank tomorrow 3pm !!! #home"), the toast says where it went, it shows in the agenda at once, the box is ready for the next one, Undo takes it back, Escape clears it and More opens the whole task with the words. Urgent and important look different in every look and mode: High priority's !!! and stripe are violet, at least 60° of hue from the look's red and readable (4.5:1) on its cards and page, so red only ever means late; a task that's late and High shows both (a violet stripe, a red border), a High one not due yet shows no red, and Home's agenda shows the same mark. The weekly review (Calendar, Week: Review unfinished N): each late task (the oldest first), each waiting on someone and each with no date, one at a time, settled with a tap (Mark done, Today, Tomorrow, Next week, Pick a day, Open, or Keep it as it is) through the same actions as everywhere else (a repeating task keeps its schedule), then a summary; a task done or deleted meanwhile is passed over, and nothing else changes.
- `bench.cjs <app folder>`: timings with the CPU slowed 4x, on a small list and a 2000-task list (`RUNS=3` for medians).
- `profile.cjs <app folder> boot|toggle|type`: where the time goes.
- `shots.cjs <app folder> <out folder> look:mode[:screen],...`: screenshots (`screen`: full, settings, task, focus, month, overdue, home, lists, calendar, week).
